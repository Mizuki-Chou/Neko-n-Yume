---

## 🌸 Where It Began

One day, at your crafting table, you made something with raw cod and sticks that you had never seen before.

It bore no name—only the print of a dozing cat, and a soft question:

> "Would you like to take a cat home?"

And so, a journey of cats and dreams quietly began.

---

## 🐾 First Meeting · The Shape of Fate

Every cat is a one-of-a-kind being.

Some are gluttons who lick the bowl spotless; some are laid-back, napping under the eaves all afternoon; some are clingy, never wanting to leave your side; and some are independent and proud, letting you pat their little head only when they are in the mood.

At birth, most are "common" children, and only a rare few are born with a "rare" sheen.

But a cat's essence is not set in stone—it is a story you write together.

Feed it a "Supreme Meow Dan". That tiny pellet, glowing with moonlight, may let it quietly transform—from common to rare, from rare to unique, and at last to the "dreamlike" form that appears only in dreams.

Every feeding is a small, heart-fluttering hope.

Remember: the starting point is written in starlight, but its final radiance is lit by the two of you.

---

## 🌙 Companionship · Treat It Gently

A cat must be raised with heart.

Feed it what it loves, gently stroke its chin, and remember to play with it. Its mood shifts like the weather—joyful, happy, calm, and sometimes low or sad.

And when you treat it gently, it will repay you with a bond that deepens day by day—from strangers to familiars, from trust to kindred souls.

When it is hungry, it will come to you. When it is happy, it will rub against you. When it is hurt, it will hide in your arms for comfort.

Please do not neglect it for too long.

A forgotten cat will grieve.

---

## ⚔️ Protection · Claws and Moonlight

Do not be fooled by its soft appearance.

When danger finds you, the cat that usually lounges about will step in front of you without hesitation.

Pounce, spirit shot, claws and moonlight—all are its weapons.

It even has tricks of its own—more than twenty active or passive "dream skills" that awaken one by one only when your hearts are in tune.

When injured, it falls into a brief "recovery", turning into a translucent shadow and quietly waiting to heal; some special children carry nine lives, or gain eternity under the moonlight.

Remember: while it protects you, it is also hoping you will turn around and give it a dried fish.

---

## 🎀 Trinkets · Gifts from Dreams

Along the way, you will meet many wonderful little things—

"Meow Dan" helps it grow gently; "XP Pills" pave the road ahead; and there are twenty-five delicate pieces of gear—collars, bells, ribbons… each hiding a small, tender magic.

Sometimes, it will even bring you a small gift.

That is the heart it musters the courage to hand you, clumsy as it is at expressing itself.

And when the two of you have lived through enough stories, a "Hall of Achievements" will quietly light up, collecting every moment worth remembering between you.

---

## 🌒 Night of the Dream Demon

On some nights, the sky is darker than usual.

The dream demon descends, and monsters grow restless and fierce.

But your cat will not be afraid—it is a child born of dreams, and the storms of the dream are its battlefield.

Take it with you, and claim the reward reserved for the brave.

---

## ✉️ Epilogue

There are no earth-shaking tales in this little world.

Only a cat, a you, and many, many peaceful days.

Welcome to the Minecraft plugin **猫と夢 · Neko n' Yume**.
