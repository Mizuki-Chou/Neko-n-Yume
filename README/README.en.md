## 🌸 The Beginning

One day, at your crafting table, you crafted something you had never seen before using raw cod and sticks.

There was no name upon it. Only a sleeping cat, and a gentle question:

> 「Would you like to take a cat home?」

And so, a journey of cats and dreams quietly began.

---

## 🐾 First Encounter · The Shape of a Bond

Every cat is a unique little soul.

Some are gluttons, licking their bowls completely clean; some are leisurely, napping beneath the eaves in the afternoon; some are clingy, never wanting to leave your side; and some are independent and proud, only allowing you to stroke their little heads when they are in the mood.

When they are first born, most are 「普通」 (Common) children. Only a very few lucky ones are born carrying the gentle glow of 「稀有」 (Rare).

But their potential is not decided at birth—it is a story you can write together.

Feed them 「至极喵丹」 (Ultimate Meow Pill). This tiny pill, shimmering with moonlight, may quietly transform them—from 「普通」 (Common) to 「稀有」 (Rare), from 「稀有」 (Rare) to 「独特」 (Unique), and finally into the 「梦幻」 (Dreamlike) form that seems to exist only within dreams.

Every feeding is a small moment of anticipation, like a heartbeat.

Remember: the beginning may be written among the stars, but the light they eventually shine is something the two of you will create together.

---

## 🌙 Companionship · With Tender Care

Cats are meant to be cared for with your heart.

Feed them what they love, gently scratch beneath their chins, and remember to spend time playing with them. Their moods change like the weather—joyful, happy, peaceful, and sometimes a little down or sad.

And when you treat them with kindness, they will return it with a bond that grows deeper day by day—from strangers, to familiar companions, to souls that understand one another completely.

When they are hungry, they will come looking for you. When they are happy, they will rub against you. And when they are hurt, they may hide in your arms seeking comfort.

Please do not leave them alone for too long.

A forgotten cat can become a very sad cat.

---

## ⚔️ Protection · Claws and Moonlight

Do not let their fluffy appearance fool you.

When danger comes, the cat that usually spends its days lounging around will not hesitate to stand between you and harm.

Pounces, spirit projectiles, claws, and moonlight—all are weapons at its disposal.

It even possesses its own little talents—more than twenty active and passive 「梦之技能」 (Dream Skills), which gradually awaken as the bond between you and your cat deepens.

When injured, it will enter a brief 「恢复期」 (Recovery Period), becoming a faint, translucent shadow as it quietly waits to heal.

Some special children may even possess nine lives, or become eternal beneath the moonlight.

Remember: even while protecting you, your cat is still hoping that you will turn around and give it a little piece of dried fish.

---

## 🎀 Little Things · Gifts from Dreams

Along your journey, you will encounter many curious little things—

「喵丹」 (Meow Pill) helps your cat grow; 「经验丸」 (Experience Orb) opens the road ahead; and there are twenty-five delicate pieces of equipment—collars, bells, ribbons, and more.

Each one holds a little bit of gentle magic.

Sometimes, your cat will even give you a small gift.

It is a little expression of affection from a creature that is not very good at putting its feelings into words.

And when you have shared enough stories together, an 「成就殿堂」 (Achievement Hall) will quietly light up, preserving every moment between you that is worth remembering.

---

## 🌒 The Night of the Dream Demon

There are nights when the sky seems a little darker than usual.

Dream Demons will descend, and monsters will become restless and powerful.

But your cat will not be afraid—it is a child born from dreams, and the storms within those dreams are its battlefield.

Take your cat with you, and claim a reward reserved for the brave.

---

## ✉️ Epilogue

This little world has no earth-shaking stories.

Only a cat, you, and many, many peaceful days together.
